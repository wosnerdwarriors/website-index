Planning

• HQ (1): Place your alliance headquarters

• Flag (2): Place a single flag

• Paint (3): Click & drag to paint flags along the mouse path

• Route (4): Click waypoints, double-click / right-click to place, Esc to cancel

• Facilities, Fortresses & Castle are loaded automatically from the map

Flag Chain

• HQ radius: 6 tiles

• Flag radius: 3 tiles

• Green border = connected flag

• Red border = disconnected flag

• Gold border = connected target

Keyboard Shortcuts

• Q = Select, W = Pan, E = Delete

• 1 = HQ, 2 = Flag/Paint/Route (press again to cycle)

• A = next alliance

• Ctrl+Z = Undo, Ctrl+Y = Redo
